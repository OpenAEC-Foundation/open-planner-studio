Open Planner Studio v2026.10 is out.

7 weeks of work: 2,177 commits, 261 pull requests and 626,000+ lines added.

New in this version:
• Open Primavera P6 .xer files directly
• Calculation profiles: calculate like P6, like MS Project, or with our own rules
• 7 new reports, including a DCMA 14-point schedule check
• Progress updates through an Excel or CSV sheet
• 28 languages, up from 14

We're looking for testers. Planners: open one of your own schedules and tell us where the dates differ from P6 or MS Project. The feedback button in the app works without an account.
