# Giving feedback

Goal: report an error or pass on an idea, optionally with a screenshot, so that it ends up as an issue on GitHub.

## When you need this

The schedule calculates something you do not understand, a button does not do what it should, or you miss something in the app. A good report helps the makers find the problem. That is why the app itself puts the version, the operating system and the language in your report. What you describe yourself decides whether they can do something with it: what you did, what you expected and what happened.

## Steps

1. Click the button *Give feedback* at the top of the window. That button is always in the title bar. Its text changes every ten minutes: *Give feedback*, *Report a bug* and *Add a new feature*. It is always the same button. The window *Send feedback* opens.
2. Choose *Bug* for something that does not work as it should, or *Feature request* for an idea. *Bug* is selected by default.
3. Fill in the *Title*. Without a title the button *Send to GitHub* is grey. Also fill in the *Description*, for example: which task you changed, what you expected and what you saw.
4. If you want to show something, turn on *Attach screenshot*. The app then makes a picture of the app without this window. A moment later you see a small preview. With *Annotate* an editor opens in which you put arrows, rectangles, freehand lines and text on the picture. There you choose the color, take back your last action with *Undo* or start over with *Clear all*. Click *Done* when you are satisfied.
5. Click *Send to GitHub*.
6. Did you not attach a screenshot? Then the GitHub page on which you create a new issue opens straight away in your browser. Continue at step 8.
7. Did you attach a screenshot? Then you first see *Almost done — your screenshot is on the clipboard.* with four steps. Click *OK, go to GitHub*, click inside the large text area of the issue page and press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac). The picture is uploaded and appears in the text.
8. At the bottom of the issue page, click *Submit new issue*. Only then is your report sent. Sign in to GitHub if you have not already.

On the issue page your title and description are already filled in. The app suggests the label *bug* or *enhancement* and puts a line under your description with the type, the version of Open Planner Studio, your operating system and the language of the app.

## Pitfalls and what the app does then

**The app sends nothing by itself.** *Send to GitHub* only opens the issue page, filled in. If you close that page without clicking *Submit new issue*, nothing has been reported. You need an account on GitHub for that.

**Your report ends up on GitHub.** Do not put anything in it that you do not want to share. That also goes for the screenshot: the picture shows everything that is visible in the app at that moment, such as task names and resources of your project. The app makes the screenshot at the moment you turn on *Attach screenshot*. So make sure the screen is right before you do that, and leave out what you do not want to show.

**Pasting does not work.** The screenshot is also saved as a file on your computer. In the desktop app the window mentions the path: *Can't paste? We also saved the screenshot to* that path *— drag that file into the text area.* In the browser the app downloads the picture as `feedback-` with a number and the extension `.png`. You find it in your downloads folder and drag it into the text area.

**The screenshot fails.** It says *Screenshot capture failed. You can still send feedback without a screenshot.* The checkbox goes off and you can send your report without a picture.

**You have typed a lot and click beside the window by accident.** That does not close the window, so your text stays. Esc or *Cancel* does close it, and then your text is gone.
