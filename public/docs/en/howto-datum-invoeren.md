# Entering a date

Goal: put a date in a date field, by typing it or by clicking it in the calendar.

## When you need this

Wherever the app asks for a date: the start or deadline of a task, a constraint, the actual start and actual finish, the status date, a holiday, the new project start, the period of a report, and the date columns in the table. If you know the date, typing is fastest. If you are looking it up ("the Monday of week 12"), click it in the calendar.

## Typing

1. Click in the field. It has three boxes for day, month and year, in the order of your date notation (*App settings*, tab *Appearance*, heading *Date format*).
2. Type the digits, for example 28, 06 and 2027. After two digits the app moves to the next box by itself. A dash, slash or full stop also moves on.
3. Press Enter, or move to another field with Tab or a click. Only then does the app take the date.

A two-digit year becomes 20xx: 6-7-26 is 6 July 2026. Pasting a whole date (for example 2027-06-28) fills all three boxes at once.

## Clicking it in the calendar

1. Click the calendar button on the right of the field. In a box, Alt+↓ works too.
2. The calendar opens on the date in the field, or on today if the field is empty. The arrows next to the month name go back or forward a month.
3. Click a day. The app takes it at once and puts the cursor back in the field.

In the calendar you see:

- the week numbers on the left, the same as in the Gantt timescale;
- today with an orange border and the chosen date filled orange;
- the days of the previous and next month in a lighter colour;
- *Today* at the bottom, which picks today's date.

The week starts on the day from *App settings*, tab *Planning*, heading *Week starts on*. Month and day names are in the language of the app.

### With the keyboard

- **Left and right arrow** — a day back or forward. In a language that reads right to left, such as Arabic, the calendar is mirrored and the left arrow is the next day.
- **Up and down arrow** — a week back or forward.
- **Home** and **End** — the first or last day of the week.
- **Page Up** and **Page Down** — a month back or forward; with Shift a year.
- **Enter** or **space** — picks the day.
- **Esc** — closes the calendar without changing anything.
- **Tab** — goes round the buttons of the calendar and the day.

## In a table cell

In the table you open a date cell with Enter or F2, or by typing. The same calendar button then sits on the right of the cell; Alt+↓ works too. A chosen day commits the cell at once, just like Enter. If the cell had a time, that time stays. Esc in the calendar only closes the calendar: the cell stays open, so you can keep typing.

## Pitfalls

**A chosen day is a change straight away.** The app takes typed text only on Enter or when you leave the field; clicking a day takes it at once. *Undo* (Ctrl+Z) puts it back.

**The calendar only picks the date.** For a field with a time next to it, a chosen day does not change the time. Type a time in the time field.

**Esc in a dialog.** While the calendar is open, Esc only closes the calendar. A second Esc closes the dialog.
