# Known pitfalls: Spanish (es)

- **usted throughout.** "su planificación", "Elija", "Haga clic en"; never tú, even where a UI hint
  uses it (see `style.note`).
- **Opening marks.** A question or exclamation opens with ¿ or ¡: "¿Por qué se mueve la fecha?"
- **Quotation marks.** «…» in the whole article, as in most of the UI; not "…" or “…”.
- **No gerund for a later action.** "La aplicación calcula las fechas, mostrando la ruta crítica" →
  "… y muestra la ruta crítica".
- **No passive with "por".** Not "es calculado por la aplicación"; write "la aplicación calcula" or
  "se calcula".
- **Lower case.** Weekdays, months and every heading word after the first: "lunes 7 de junio de
  2027", "Agregar relaciones entre tareas". Decimal comma (0,5 días); WBS codes such as 1.2 keep the
  period.
- **False friends.** "actual" = current (actual start is "inicio real", see `terms`); "eventual" =
  possible (eventually → "finalmente"); "librería" = bookshop (→ "biblioteca"); "soportar" = to bear
  (→ "admitir", "ser compatible con"); "asumir" = to take on, not to suppose (→ "suponer").
- **"en su lugar" means instead.** For "in place" write "definida": "antes de que la lógica esté
  definida", not "esté en su lugar".
- **Word senses.** desfase is only the lag on a relation; a late task "se retrasa un día". A house
  extension is ampliación, not extensión. The break in the working day is pausa (`terms`), not
  interrupción.
- **Calques.** "corre tarde" (runs late) → "se retrasa"; "hace sentido" → "tiene sentido"; "como si
  fuera" (as it were) → "por así decirlo".
