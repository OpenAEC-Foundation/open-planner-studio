# Known pitfalls: Swedish (sv)

- **Compounds in one word.** "projektkalender", "standardvärde", "uppgiftstabell"; never split
  ("projekt kalender"). After an abbreviation or a code use a hyphen: "IFC-fil", "P6-projekt".
- **Double definiteness.** An adjective before a definite noun needs den/det/de and the suffix: "den
  kritiska linjen", "det nya sambandet", "de efterföljande uppgifterna". Wrong: "den kritiska linje".
- **en or ett.** "en uppgift", "en baslinje", "ett samband", "ett slack", "ett projekt", "ett datum"
  (see `style.note`). Adjectives and pronouns agree: "ett nytt samband", "nya samband", "inget slack".
- **inte in a subordinate clause.** inte stands before the verb: "om uppgiften inte har något slack",
  not "om uppgiften har inte något slack". A main clause keeps the verb second: "Då flyttar appen
  uppgiften", not "Då appen flyttar".
- **din, ditt, dina.** Agree with the noun: "din tidsplan", "ditt projekt", "dina uppgifter".
- **Dates and numbers.** "måndag 7 juni 2027": weekday and month in lower case, no period after the
  day. Decimal comma (0,5 dagar); WBS codes such as 1.2 keep the period.
- **False friends.** "aktuell" = current (`style.docs` uses it right: "inte längre aktuell"); actual
  is "faktisk"; "eventuell" = possible, not eventual.
- **Word senses.** A late task "blir försenad" or "blir klar en dag senare"; fördröjning is only the
  lag on a relation. A house extension is tillbyggnad, not tillägg. The break in the working day is
  rast (`terms`), not avbrott.
- **Calques.** "springer sent" (runs late) → "blir försenad"; "på plats" (in place) → "klar" or
  "inlagd"; "faller tillbaka till" (falls back to) → "sätts till".
