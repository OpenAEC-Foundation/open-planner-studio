# Known pitfalls: Japanese (ja)

- **です/ます in every sentence.** No plain だ/である endings in running text. A list item that is only
  a noun phrase may end without です.
- **Counters.** As in the UI: "3 件のタスク", "2 稼働日", "8 時間"; never a bare digit before a counted
  noun ("3 タスク").
- **Spaces around Latin letters and digits.** One half-width space between Japanese and Latin
  letters or digits, and around an italic label, as in `style.docs` ("*保存* を選択します"). No space
  inside a date (2027年6月7日).
- **Full-width punctuation.** 、。（） and 「…」 for quotations, as in the UI; no ASCII , . ( ) and no
  “…” in Japanese sentences.
- **Long vowel mark.** Katakana words keep the final ー, as in `terms`: "サマリータスク", "カレンダー",
  "ユーザー"; not "ユーザ".
- **ことができます.** "変更することができます" → "変更できます".
- **No それ or あなた for every "it" and "your".** Repeat the noun or leave it out.
- **Long sentences.** Split an English sentence with several clauses. Put the condition first:
  "先行タスクが複数ある場合、タスクは最後の先行タスクが終了するまで待ちます。"
- **Dates.** "2027年6月7日（月）"; times as in the source (07:00). Decimal point, as in English.
- **Word senses.** A late task "1 日遅れて終了します". A house extension is 増築, not 拡張機能. The break
  in the working day is 休憩 (`terms`), not 中断.
- **Calques.** "その場で" (in place) → "設定済み"; "遅れて走ります" (runs late) → "遅れます"; "いわば" is
  right for "as it were".
