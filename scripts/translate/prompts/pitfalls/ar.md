# Known pitfalls: Arabic (ar)

- **Number and noun agreement.** 3–10 take a plural noun in the genitive, 11–99 a singular accusative,
  100+ a singular genitive. Wrong: "2 أيام", right: "يوما عمل" or "يومان". Check every digit
  followed by a noun.
- **The dual needs no digit.** "يومان", "ساعتان" already say two. Do not add "(2)" after a dual.
  Where the source has the digit 2, keep it as a digit and write the noun so that it agrees.
- **No demonstrative before a digit.** Not "هذه 4 ساعات"; write "هذه الساعات الأربع" or restructure
  the sentence. The check counts digits: keep the digit 4 somewhere in the sentence if the source has it.
- **Jussive after لم.** The verb drops its long vowel: "إذا لم ترَها", not "إذا لم تراها".
- **Elative constructions.** "أحدث النهاية المبكرة" is broken; write "أبعد نهاية مبكرة بين جميع المهام".
- **Word senses that mislead.** الافتراض = assumption, not default (use الافتراضي / الوحدة
  الافتراضية); مهمة اليوم = today's task, not day task (المهمة بالأيام); a Gantt bar is شريط, never
  عمود (that is a table column); a lunch break is استراحة, not الانقطاع (the task interruption).
- **Direction of a relation.** Check who comes first: "المهمة الموجزة التي تندرج تحتها" (the task sits
  under its summary task), not the reverse.
- **One spelling and one quotation style.** Tanween on the alif as ـًا (أيضًا, مثلًا) everywhere; one
  pair of quotation marks (see `style.quotes`).
- **Calques.** "في وضع تشغيل" → "عند التفعيل"; "فقط" after the verb for 'only' → "لا … إلا";
  "يوم عمل واحد أكثر" → "متأخرًا يوم عمل واحدًا".
