# Known pitfalls: Korean (ko)

- **합니다/하세요 only.** No 해요 forms ("변경해요") and no plain forms ("변경한다") in running text.
- **Particles after the last syllable.** A final consonant takes 을, 이, 은, 과, 으로; a vowel takes 를,
  가, 는, 와, 로 (ㄹ also takes 로): "*저장*을", "*삭제*를", "작업이", "관계가", "날짜로", "파일로".
  After a code, follow its pronunciation: "F5를", "IFC를".
- **Spacing.** Dependent nouns stand apart: "할 수 있습니다", "변경할 때", "계산한 것"; not
  "할수있습니다".
- **No double passive.** "계산되어집니다" → "계산됩니다"; "보여집니다" → "표시됩니다".
- **No 그것 for "it".** Repeat the noun ("이 작업") or leave it out.
- **Long sentences.** Split an English sentence with several clauses. Put the condition first with
  -(으)면 or -는 경우: "선행 작업이 여러 개인 경우 작업은 마지막 선행 작업이 끝날 때까지 기다립니다."
- **Quotation marks.** “…” in the whole article, as in most of the UI; not ‘…’ or "…".
- **Dates.** "2027년 6월 7일 월요일"; times as in the source (07:00). Decimal point, as in English.
- **Word senses.** 지연 is the lag on a relation; a late task "하루 늦게 끝납니다", not "하루 지연됩니다".
  A house extension is 증축, not 확장 프로그램. The break in the working day is 휴식 (`terms`), not 중단.
- **Calques.** "제자리에" (in place) → "설정되어 있으면"; "늦게 달립니다" (runs late) → "늦어집니다";
  "말하자면" is right for "as it were".
