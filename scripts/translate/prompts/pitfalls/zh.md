# Known pitfalls: Chinese, Simplified (zh)

- **Full-width punctuation.** ，。：；？！（） and “…” in Chinese sentences; no ASCII , . : ; ( ) or
  "…" between Chinese characters.
- **Spaces around Latin letters and digits.** One space between Chinese characters and Latin letters
  or digits, as in the UI: "按 F5", "已选择 3 个任务". No space next to full-width punctuation and none
  inside a date (2027年6月7日).
- **Measure words.** A digit before a countable noun needs a measure word, as in the UI: "2 个任务",
  "3 个工作日"; "8 小时" and "2 天" take none. Wrong: "2 任务".
- **No plural marking.** Do not add 们 or 一些 for an English plural: "任务们" is wrong.
- **被 sparingly.** "日期被应用计算" → "应用会计算日期". Use 被 only when something bad happens to the
  subject.
- **的 chains.** At most two 的 in one noun phrase. Split a long English relative clause into its own
  sentence.
- **进行.** Not as a filler: "进行计算" → "计算"; "对任务进行排序" → "给任务排序".
- **Simplified characters only.** No traditional forms: 時間 → 时间, 關係 → 关系, 計算 → 计算.
- **Dates.** "2027年6月7日（星期一）"; times as in the source (07:00). Decimal point, as in English.
- **Word senses.** A late task "推迟一天完成". A house extension is 扩建, not 扩展 (the software
  extension). The break in the working day is 休息 (`terms`), not 中断.
- **Calques.** "在适当位置" (in place) → "已设置好"; "可以这么说" (as it were) → "相当于"; "运行晚了"
  (runs late) → "延误".
