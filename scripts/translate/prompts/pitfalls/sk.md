# Known pitfalls: Slovak (sk)

- **Slovak, not Czech.** No ř, ě, ů. Czech words that slip in: "úkol" → "úloha", "který" → "ktorý",
  "jsou" → "sú", "není" → "nie je", "pouze" → "iba/len", "také" → "tiež", "dny" → "dni".
- **Clitics in the second position.** *sa, si, ho, mu, by, som* stand after the first stressed unit:
  "Potom sa každá úloha počíta ako kritická", not "Potom každá úloha sa počíta".
- **Numerals.** 2–4 + nominative plural, 5 and more + genitive plural, a decimal + genitive singular:
  "2 pracovné dni", "5 pracovných dní", "1,5 dňa". Gender: "dve úlohy", "dva dni".
- **Negation.** "nie je" is two words; other verbs join ne-: "nemá". *žiadny, nič, nikdy* need a
  negated verb: "žiadna úloha nemá časovú rezervu", not "žiadna úloha má".
- **so, zo, vo, ku.** The vowel form only before a similar consonant: "so stĺpcom", "zo zoznamu",
  "vo vlastnostiach", "ku koncu"; otherwise "s dátumom", "z kalendára", "v okne".
- **calculate in prose.** *prepočítať* is the command. A sentence that only says the app works
  something out uses "počíta" or "určí": "čo aplikácia počíta z vašich závislostí".
- **Imperative only in main clauses.** After *ktorý, v ktorom* use the indicative: "okno, v ktorom si
  necháte vygenerovať plán", not "nechajte".
- **Dates and numbers.** "v pondelok 7. júna 2027": month in the genitive, lower case. Decimal comma
  (0,5); WBS codes such as 1.2 keep the period.
- **Example names take no endings.** A carrier noun takes the case: "trvanie úlohy *Place crane*", not
  "trvanie *Place crane*u".
- **Word senses.** A task that runs late "mešká" or "skončí o deň neskôr"; oneskorenie is only the lag
  on a relation. A house extension is prístavba, not rozšírenie. prestávka is the termbase word for
  both the working-day break and the task interruption: not an error.
- **Calques.** "beží neskoro" → "mešká"; "na mieste" (in place) → "zadané"; "akoby" (as it were) →
  "v podstate".
